Our Services

What We Specialize In

  • Mobile Telematics Installations

    Professional on-site installation of telematics devices, cameras, and sensors for all vehicle types—performed by certified, reliable technicians.

  • Fleet Electronics Solutions

    End-to-end electronic systems integration—from GPS tracking and ELDs to in-cab communications—for fleets of any scale or configuration.

  • Consultative Solution Design

    Expert consultation to plan, scope, and optimize fleet technology deployments—ensuring your investment aligns with workflow and compliance goals.

  • Device Sales

    Vetted hardware and accessories with expert guidance to ensure you get the right tools for your fleet's specific needs.

  • System Upgrades & Retrofits

    Modernize and standardize your fleet with the latest technology, ensuring compatibility and performance across all vehicles.

  • Project Management

    Professional coordination for multi-site rollouts to ensure seamless execution, on-time delivery, and minimal downtime.

  • Maintenance & Support

    Troubleshooting, swap-outs, and preventive checks to keep your fleet running smoothly and minimize operational disruptions.

  • Training & Education

    Hands-on training and standard operating procedures (SOPs) to empower your team and ensure proper system usage.

  • Compliance Services

    Configurations and documentation for audits to ensure regulatory compliance and avoid costly penalties.

  • Specialized Installations

    Complex vehicles and custom upfits tailored to your unique needs, handled by experts in specialized fleet requirements.
